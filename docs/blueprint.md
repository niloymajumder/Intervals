# **App Name**: Intervals Studio

## Core Features:

- Home Page: A landing page that introduces Intervals’ vision and philosophy using minimal text, generous spacing, and subtle motion.
- Philosophy Section: Dedicated section explaining the Intervals mindset: cycles, pauses, listening, long-term thinking, and human rhythm.
- Products Showcase: Sections/cards introducing Thirty Magazine and Anantitya, designed as independent identities under one ecosystem.
- Studio/Company Explanation: Explains Intervals as a mother company, detailing how ideas are built, paused, refined, and repeated into products.
- Future Speculation: A quiet, speculative section hinting at upcoming tech-based products and experiments.
- Team Introduction: Minimal introduction to the people and roles behind Intervals.
- Contact/Collaboration Inquiry: Simple, calm inquiry section for contact and collaboration.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft beige (#F0F0E0) to evoke a sense of calm and minimalism.
- Background color: Off-white (#FAFAFA) to enhance the use of negative space.
- Accent color: Muted gray (#A0A0A0) for subtle highlights and interactive elements.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif, to give a modern and neutral feel.
- Modern layout, inspired by leading studio websites, with a focus on clean lines and structured content presentation.
- Editorial-style grid layout with large margins and generous negative space to provide breathing room and enhance clarity.
- Soft micro-animations, such as fades, slides, and parallax-lite effects, to reflect cycles and pauses in a subtle manner.
- Use simple, geometric icons that complement the modern sans-serif typography.
